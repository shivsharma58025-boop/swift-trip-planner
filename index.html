How Rotation Fairness Works

1. Every destination has a complexity weight (e.g. Kolkata = 1.0, Haldia Dock = 1.3).
2. A traveller's Weighted Load = sum of all assigned trip weights this month.
3. When a new WhatsApp or Sheet trip is created, the system auto-allots it to the active staff with the lowest load.
4. Staff on approved leave or already assigned on overlapping dates are skipped.
5. The Fairness Gap keeps allotment balanced within ±1.0 weight unit.
